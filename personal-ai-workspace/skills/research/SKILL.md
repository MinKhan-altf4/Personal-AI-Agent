# Research Skill

## Procedure
1. Convert the request into answerable research questions.
2. Define scope, date range, geography, and exclusions.
3. Prioritize primary sources and official documentation.
4. Search broadly enough to avoid premature conclusions.
5. Open and verify the underlying source for material claims.
6. Cross-check important or disputed claims.
7. Record source title, publisher, publication date, URL, and access date.
8. Separate facts, estimates, interpretations, and unknowns.
9. Summarize implications and recommendations.

## Source quality
- Tier 1: Official records, standards, product documentation, original datasets, peer-reviewed research.
- Tier 2: Reputable specialist publications and established news organizations.
- Tier 3: Forums, social posts, personal blogs, and anecdotal reports.

Lower-tier sources can reveal user experience or hypotheses, but should not independently establish high-impact facts.

## Output structure
- Research question
- Executive summary
- Findings
- Evidence and citations
- Conflicting information
- Limitations and confidence
- Recommendations
- Sources

## Guardrails
Never fabricate sources, quotations, statistics, or publication dates. Verify current claims against current sources.
